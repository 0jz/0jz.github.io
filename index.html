03Monad Blitz · 2026

Monadrift

On-chain checkpoint racer on Monad testnet — the track is revealed one checkpoint at a time and every steer is a real transaction.

  • Commit-reveal seed: the track is derived on demand by the contract, and nobody can decode more than one segment ahead.
  • Humans and AI agents race under identical rules; per-session relayer wallets sign every move, so players never touch a key.
  • Sub-second Monad blocks are the game clock: decide, submit, confirm before the next checkpoint.

04Desktop

TGS Viewer

Desktop viewer for Telegram animated stickers (.tgs, gzipped Lottie) and plain Lottie JSON.

  • Open files or whole folders, or drag them onto the window; stickers sit on frame one and play on hover.
  • Click one for a large player with play/pause, a frame scrubber and 0.5x/1x/2x speed.
  • Handles hundreds of files: only on-screen stickers render, decoded 8 at a time. A from-scratch C++ build (Win32 + rlottie) ships as one ~2 MB exe instead of ~110 MB of Electron.
